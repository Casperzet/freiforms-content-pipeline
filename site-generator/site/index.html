Questions we hear most

How fast can we start?

After the free call and a short analysis we agree scope and a fixed price. A first working version is usually a matter of weeks, not months.

Do my people need to install an app?

No. It opens in the phone browser and can be added to the home screen. No app store, no accounts to manage on your side.

Where is our data kept?

We agree the hosting region with you before the start. For customers in the EU we use hosting in the EU.

Does it follow our labor rules?

Hours and breaks are counted against limits you set, so planning stays inside your rules. This is a planning aid, not legal advice. Please check local labor law and agreements.

What does the free website include?

With prepayment of the Growth plan we build you a modern website with booking or request forms. Details are written down in the offer before you pay.
